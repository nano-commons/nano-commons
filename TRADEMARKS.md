# Name and Trademark Policy

The names **"Nano-Commons"** and **"Nano-Commons Protocol"**, and any project logo, belong to the commons. They exist so that people can tell the real, open, token-free project apart from imitations. Whoever holds them does so only as a custodian for the community (see [GOVERNANCE.md §7](GOVERNANCE.md)).

The code and documents are free to use under their licenses. **This policy covers only the names.**

## You may, without asking

- **Say what is true.** For example, "built on the Nano-Commons Protocol", "compatible with Nano-Commons" or "a fork of Nano-Commons".
- **Refer to the project** in articles, research, teaching, talks and news.
- **Run your own node console** and say that it runs Nano-Commons software.
- **Publish an unmodified copy** of an official release under its name.

## You may not

- **Imply that you are the project.** Do not suggest that you are the official Nano-Commons project, network, foundation or service, or that you are endorsed by it.
- **Attach the name to anything financial.** That includes a token, coin, NFT, investment or any other financial product. **There is no Nano-Commons token, and there never will be.**
- **Use the name for a central or hosted service** that presents itself as "the" Nano-Commons service. The charter rules out central servers.
- **Use the name for a modified version** without making clear that it is modified and unofficial.
- **Register the name** as a domain, company name, social account or trademark that could be confused with the project.

## Forks

Forks are welcome. A fork must:
- use its own name,
- use its own `network_id`,
- say clearly that it is based on the Nano-Commons Protocol.

## Status

**The name is not registered, and no one may register it as private property.** That includes the initiator. These rules rest on:
- public, dated use of the name by the commons,
- the licenses' terms (AGPL section 7(e), see [NOTICE](NOTICE)),
- good faith.

**Defensive registration, by consensus only.** A registration is allowed only if someone tries to claim the name, or to use it for impersonation or a token scam. It must then be decided by consensus as a charter change ([GOVERNANCE.md §5](GOVERNANCE.md)), filed solely to protect the commons, and held in trust for it, never by or for any individual.

Questions go to the stewards listed in [STEWARDS.md](STEWARDS.md).
