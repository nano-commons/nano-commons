## Code: AGPL-3.0-or-later

**You may:** use, study, change and share the software for any purpose, including commercially.

**You must:**
- Keep the license, copyright notices and attribution (see [NOTICE](NOTICE)).
- Release your changes under the same license when you distribute the software.
- **Offer the source of your modified version to its users** when you run it as a network service. This is the clause that stops anyone from turning the commons into a closed hosted product.

**You may not:** use the project's names or marks in a way that suggests endorsement or official status (see [TRADEMARKS.md](TRADEMARKS.md)).

## Documents: CC BY-SA 4.0

**You may:** share and adapt the documents for any purpose, including commercially.

**You must:**
- **Give credit.** Name the initiator and contributors (for example, "Nano-Commons Protocol, initiated by Krittidet Chanthake, and contributors"), link to the license and say whether you made changes.
- **Share alike.** Release your adaptations under CC BY-SA 4.0 or a compatible license.

**You may not:** present the documents, or adaptations of them, as your own original work without that credit.
