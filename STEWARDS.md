## Bootstrap status

**1 steward from 1 organisation.**

Until the project has **at least 3 stewards from at least 2 unrelated organisations or communities**, only routine changes and security fixes can be adopted ([GOVERNANCE.md §5.3](GOVERNANCE.md)). Protocol changes, charter changes and decisions about the project's name and assets wait until then.

## Becoming a steward

Open a public proposal that names the candidate and links to their contributions. The proposal is decided by consensus like any other change ([GOVERNANCE.md §4.1](GOVERNANCE.md)).
